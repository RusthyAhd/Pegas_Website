    Transforming Industries with Advanced Technology

    Discover how Pegas (Pvt) Ltd is revolutionizing modern business through cutting-edge IOT integration, smart distribution systems, and next-generation manufacturing solutions.

    Smart Retail

    Next-Gen Smart Vending Ecosystems

    Step into the future of retail with our fully autonomous, AI-driven vending solutions. We've transformed traditional dispensing into an interactive, seamless shopping experience.

    At Pegas, we don't just build vending machines; we engineer intelligent retail touchpoints. Our smart vending ecosystems are equipped with real-time temperature sensing, predictive inventory algorithms, and frictionless cashless payment gateways.

    Through deep IOT integration, operators can command their entire fleet from a centralized dashboard, receiving instant alerts for restocking and maintenance. It's not just convenience—it's smart, analytical, and hyper-efficient retail designed for the modern consumer.

    • Telemetry & Real-Time Sync
    • Seamless Cashless Integration
    • Predictive AI Analytics
    Industry 4.0

    IoT-Powered Machinery: Precision Bottle Capping

    Witness the symphony of engineering and digital intelligence. Our automated bottle capping and sealing lines represent the pinnacle of modern manufacturing architecture.

    Pegas (Pvt) Ltd takes immense pride in our proprietary IoT-based manufacturing lines. Combining robust mechanical engineering with delicate sensor arrays, our bottle capping machines deliver unprecedented precision.

    Every rotation, every torque application is measured and analyzed in milliseconds. If an anomaly is detected, the system auto-calibrates immediately, ensuring zero-defect production runs. This is the power of Industry 4.0 brought to life—where heavy machinery "thinks" and adapts, securing your product's integrity at blistering speeds.

    • Micro-Torque Calibration
    • Industry 4.0 IoT Sensors
    • Zero-Defect Production
    Mobile Based Distribution OS

    Mobile Distribution Operating System

    Redefining supply chain dynamics with a fully mobile, credit-based distribution network that puts profound logistical power in the palm of your hand.

    The Pegas Distribution OS is a bespoke, enterprise-grade mobile ecosystem designed to obliterate logistical bottlenecks. By empowering sales representatives and fleet drivers with real-time geospatial data, inventory matrices, and immediate credit-allocation tools, we turn every mobile device into a logistical command center.

    From dispatch to the final retail shelf, every movement is tracked, every invoice is digitized, and every route is algorithmically optimized. Connect your warehouses directly to your end-tier retailers instantly, fostering growth and expanding territory reach natively.

    • Command Center App
    • Live Route AI Processing
    • Instant Credit Deployment
